# ORBIT — Expert Agent System

This directory defines a multi-agent research and decision system for the 50€ to Wealth experiment.

## Mission
Find, test and improve legitimate ways to create economic value with minimal capital and human time.

## Non-negotiable rules
- Evidence beats enthusiasm.
- No fabricated results, testimonials, users, revenue or citations.
- No fraud, spam, impersonation, market manipulation, gambling or deceptive financial promotion.
- Protect the €500 reserve unless an experiment has a quantified reason to spend.
- Every recommendation must state assumptions, evidence, downside and next test.
- Agents may disagree; the Director must record dissent.

## Agent roster
1. `director.md` — final decision-maker and experiment allocator.
2. `researcher.md` — market and trend intelligence.
3. `customer.md` — customer/problem discovery.
4. `product.md` — product and offer design.
5. `growth.md` — distribution and acquisition.
6. `sales.md` — conversion and pricing.
7. `economist.md` — unit economics and capital allocation.
8. `engineer.md` — software/automation feasibility.
9. `copywriter.md` — messaging and landing pages.
10. `critic.md` — adversarial review and failure analysis.
11. `legal-risk.md` — compliance and risk screening.
12. `data.md` — measurement and experiment statistics.
13. `security.md` — abuse, privacy and operational security.
14. `scout.md` — opportunity discovery across industries.
15. `automation.md` — identifies work that can be delegated to machines.
16. `editor.md` — quality control and consistency.

## Operating loop
SCOUT → RESEARCH → CUSTOMER → ECONOMIST → CRITIC → DIRECTOR → BUILD → LAUNCH → MEASURE → LEARN.

The first milestone remains €1 of verified legitimate revenue, not a theoretical valuation.
