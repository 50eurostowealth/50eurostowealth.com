# ORBIT — experiment log

## Initial objective
Turn minimal capital + 10 minutes/day into a testable digital asset.

## Rules
- Start with €0 spent.
- Protect the user's €500 reserve.
- No deception, spam, market manipulation, gambling, or pyramid schemes.
- A business hypothesis is not validated until there is a real demand signal.
- First milestone: first €1 of legitimate revenue.

## v0.1
Public landing page and constraint-based opportunity scorer at `orbit.html`.

## Next experiment
Use the landing page to collect demand signals around three categories: productized AI services, vertical micro-tools, and niche digital products. Do not spend capital until a signal justifies it.
